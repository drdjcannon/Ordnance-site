# Ordnance: Military Systems

**A reference catalogue of military systems of the world, in your pocket and offline.**

Ordnance covers more than ten thousand ships, aircraft, land systems, missiles and satellites from 126
countries, in service from 1914 to today. Three hundred American systems are written up in depth, with
photographs and an editorial note on why each one matters; the rest of the world comes from Wikidata
and Wikipedia.

- **Drawn to scale.** Put a carrier beside a destroyer, or a Spitfire beside a B-52, and every outline
  is drawn to one common scale, with a football field or a city bus for reference.
- **Search everything.** Designations, nicknames and countries: "fa18" finds the F/A-18E, "Warthog"
  finds the A-10.
- **One table for every entry**, in feet or metres, with "Not recorded" wherever the source is silent,
  never a guess.
- **Service life timeline**: see which aircraft outlived the ones meant to replace them.
- **Nine themes**, Dynamic Type throughout, and no account, advertising or tracking.
