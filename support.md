# Ordnance – Support

## Getting started

- **Home** is a search field, a spotlight of well-known systems, and shortcuts such as Fighters,
  Submarines and Artillery. Type a designation, a name, a nickname or a country.
- **Sea, Land, Air and Space** each list their domain, with filters for type, status, era and operator.
- **Compare size** on any entry adds it to the scale comparison (up to six). The ruler at the top of Home
  opens it.
- **Library** holds what you have saved and recently viewed, and the service life timeline.
- **Settings** (the gear on Home) has units, theme, scale references, haptics, and clearing your data.

## Common questions

**Do I need an account, or the internet?**
No. The catalogue, the text and the photographs are all on your device, and the app works offline.
Links to Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons open in Safari.

**Where do the facts come from?**
The three hundred curated entries are written for the app. Everything else comes from Wikidata, and the
summaries and specification tables from English Wikipedia, credited on every page that uses them.
Photographs come from Wikimedia Commons and US government archives, each with its licence and credit.

**I found a wrong figure.**
Please tell us. The figures are drawn from public sources and have not all been checked against primary
documents, which the app says plainly. A field we cannot verify is left as "Not recorded" rather than
guessed, but published sources do disagree.

**Why can I not compare this system?**
A system needs a published length to be drawn to scale. Where none exists, the app says so.

**Is this affiliated with any government or armed force?**
No. Ordnance is an independent reference work. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the US
Department of Defense or any other government, and it sells nothing but optional tips.

**What does a tip get me?**
A thank-you. Tips are optional and unlock nothing: every feature is included for everyone.
