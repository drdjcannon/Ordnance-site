# Ordnance – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 12 November 2026_

**Ordnance collects nothing about you.**

## Everything stays on your device

The app makes no network requests of its own. The catalogue, the Wikipedia text and every photograph are
built into the app. Your saved systems, recently viewed list, comparison and settings are stored only on
your device, and deleting the app deletes them.

There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking. The developer cannot see
what you search for, read or save.

## Links

Some pages link to the Wikipedia article or the Wikimedia Commons file they draw on. Tapping one opens
Safari, and those sites' own privacy policies apply from there.

## Sharing

When you share a system, the app hands a short text summary to the share sheet. Where it goes from there
is your choice.

## Tips

Optional tips are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer receives no payment details and
no personal information.

## Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.
